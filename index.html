ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE & SOCIAL MEDIA

Deepfakes: Can You Trust What You See?

How AI-generated media is changing trust, safety and information on social media.

Explore the Issue

INTRODUCTION

What Are Deepfakes?

Deepfakes are images, videos or audio that are created or changed using AI to make them look real. They are becoming more common on social media and can be used for entertainment, but they can also be used to spread false information.


Because deepfakes can look very realistic, people may find it difficult to know what is true and what is fake. This can affect how much people trust the information they see online. Deepfakes may also cause problems such as misinformation, scams and damage to a person's reputation.

Sources: MIT Sloan & Lancaster University

RESEARCH QUESTION

To what extent and in what ways do deepfakes influence people's trust in information on social media?

01 — THE ISSUE

The Problem

Deepfakes can make false videos, images and audio appear realistic, making it harder for people to decide whether online information is true or false.

01

Misinformation

Deepfakes can be used to spread false information. Because social media content can spread very quickly, a deepfake may reach many people before it is identified as false.

02

Loss of Trust

Deepfakes can create two problems: people may believe fake information, but they may also begin to doubt real information because they know AI can manipulate videos and recordings.

03

Scams & Impersonation

Deepfake videos and AI-generated voices can be used to impersonate people, support scams and damage someone's reputation.

WHY THIS MATTERS

As deepfake technology becomes more realistic, social media users may find it increasingly difficult to know which sources and information they can trust.

KEY ANALYSIS

Two Ways Deepfakes Damage Trust

My research suggests that deepfakes can damage trust in two different ways.

01

Believe the Fake

A realistic deepfake may convince someone that false information is genuine. This can influence opinions, decisions and beliefs before the content is identified as misleading.

+
02

Doubt the Real

Awareness of deepfakes may also make people suspicious of genuine videos, images and audio. Real evidence can therefore be questioned simply because people know that convincing fake media can be created.

RESULT

Both effects can reduce confidence in information on social media and create a wider atmosphere of uncertainty.

02 — AI'S ROLE

How Does AI Create a Deepfake?

AI systems can analyse images, videos and audio to learn patterns from a person's face, voice, expressions and movements.

AI Can Also Detect Deepfakes

AI is not only used to create deepfakes. Detection systems can analyse media for signs of manipulation or unusual patterns. However, detection is not always completely accurate, especially when new AI models are developed.

04 — POSITIVE USES

Benefits of the Technology

Although deepfake technology can create risks, it can also have useful applications when it is used responsibly and with consent.

Entertainment

Deepfake technology can help create realistic digital performances and reduce the need for actors to repeatedly film or record the same content.

Source: WIPO

Translation & Dubbing

WIPO gives the example of David Beckham using AI-related technology to deliver a campaign message in nine different languages.

Source: WIPO

Accessibility

Voice cloning can help people who have lost their ability to speak. Technology has also been used to recreate voices for assistive communication.

Sources: WIPO & Soundverse

05 — RISKS

Risks & Challenges

Privacy

A person's face, voice or identity may be used without their permission. Deepfakes can also be used for blackmail, embarrassment or reputation damage.

Fraud & Security

Deepfakes can support impersonation scams, identity theft and unauthorised transactions. AI voice cloning can imitate family members, colleagues or business leaders.

Sources: MAS & McAfee

Detection

Detection systems may perform well on deepfakes they already recognise but struggle with new or unfamiliar generators. Compression, resizing and editing can also make detection harder.

Source: DuckDuckGoose AI

Reputation & Law

Harmful deepfakes can damage a person's reputation. Current laws may not always fully address the creation and sharing of harmful synthetic media.

Source: Mishcon de Reya

WHO IS AFFECTED?

Deepfakes Affect More Than One Group

The impact of deepfakes can reach individuals, businesses, public figures and society as a whole.

Social Media Users

Users may be misled by false videos or become less certain about whether online information is genuine.

Businesses

Companies may face impersonation scams, financial losses, data theft and damage to their reputation.

Public Figures

Politicians, celebrities and other public figures may have their face or voice used in fake media that changes how people view them.

Society

If people become unsure whether digital evidence is real, trust in news, social media and public institutions may decrease.

06 — SOCIETY

Impact on Society

Deepfakes can affect individuals and society by changing how people communicate, trust information and make decisions. On social media, they can spread quickly and make it difficult for users to know whether videos, images or audio are genuine.


Research published in the International Journal on Informatics Visualization suggests that deepfakes can reduce trust in news and social media. Media literacy is therefore important because it can help people think more critically about the information they see online.

Source: International Journal on Informatics Visualization


Deepfakes can also influence politics and public opinion. Realistic fake videos or audio of politicians may affect how people view public figures and may reduce trust in politicians, media and public institutions.

Source: Taylor's University


Businesses can also be affected. Deepfake voices and videos can be used to impersonate company leaders and convince employees to transfer money or reveal sensitive information.

Source: UNITED VARS


Overall, deepfakes can create a wider atmosphere of doubt. People may not only believe false information, but may also begin to question genuine information because they know digital content can be manipulated.

07 — ETHICS & SAFETY

Ethical & Safety Considerations

Deepfakes raise ethical and safety concerns because they can use a person's face, voice or personal data without permission. This creates privacy and data protection risks, especially when information from social media is used to train AI systems.

Source: LexisNexis


Deepfakes can also be used for misinformation, harassment, blackmail, bias, identity theft and impersonation. These problems can harm individuals and reduce trust in online information.

Source: Observer Research Foundation


Responsible AI use is therefore important. Measures such as consent, content labels, watermarking and stronger platform rules can help reduce harmful uses of deepfake technology.

08 — SOLUTIONS

What Can Be Done?

01

Individuals

People should verify suspicious videos, audio and investment messages before trusting or sharing them. They should also avoid oversharing personal photos, videos and voice recordings.

02

Businesses

KPMG recommends employee training, stronger identity checks, continuous verification, multi-factor authentication and investment in detection and response tools.

Source: KPMG

03

Social Media Platforms

Platforms can detect suspicious content, label AI-generated media, remove harmful deepfakes and improve transparency. Meta has introduced labels for AI-generated content.

Source: Meta

04

Content Verification

C2PA Content Credentials can provide information about where digital content came from and how it has been edited. This can help users better understand the history of an image or video.

Source: C2PA

05

Governments

Governments can strengthen laws, improve public education and digital literacy, and require platforms to respond more quickly to harmful deepfakes. Clearer AI-specific rules could also help reduce scams and identity misuse.

FINAL CONCLUSION

So, How Much Do Deepfakes Affect Trust?

Deepfakes can influence people's trust in social media information to a significant extent.

Their impact is not limited to making people believe false information. Deepfakes can also make people doubt genuine videos, images and audio because users know that realistic media can now be created or manipulated using AI.


The level of influence can depend on the audience, the realism of the deepfake, the source of the content and how quickly it spreads. Research such as the Ahmed et al. study also suggests that the effect may not be identical for every audience.


Overall, the greatest danger is a wider loss of confidence in online information. For this reason, media literacy, verification, responsible AI use, platform transparency and stronger security measures are important for reducing the harm caused by deepfakes.
